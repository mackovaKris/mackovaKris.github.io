I'm a back-end software engineer with five years of experience building fleet and logistics management systems at Eurowag. My work entails not only hands-on engineering but also active contribution to solution design — shaping how systems should be built before and while they are built.

I understand the needs of delivering fast, or delivering lasting quality. I ask questions, surface trade-offs, and keep the bigger picture in mind. I believe being transparent about effort, cost, and fidelity leads to better decisions — whether that means flagging a quick solution's limitations or making the case for a more robust approach.

Examples of my recent work: rebuilding legacy systems on optimized, scalable architectures. This includes a real-time logistics matching system capable of pairing live vehicle capacity with transport orders across 20,000 live vehicles, designed to scale to one million. My core stack is .NET, Kubernetes, Kafka, Azure Event Hubs, Cosmos DB, and PostgreSQL, with DevOps practices and AI-assisted workflows throughout.

I believe collaboration is at the heart of good software development. I support an environment where people ask questions freely, challenge decisions, and defend their own thinking. Conversation produces better code and stronger solutions. I try to model this myself through code reviews and always being available to support my colleagues.

I graduated from the [Faculty of Informatics and Information Technologies at the Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava](https://www.fiit.stuba.sk) in 2020. Outside of professional work, I have an interest in game development and have dabbled in VR development.

And who am I as a person? I read a lot, enjoy an evening of video games or board games with friends, and have a really cool dog.
